	1.0 Purpose
	1.0 目的
	The Ontario Heritage Bridge Guidelines (OHBG) are designed to provide direction on the conservation of provincially owned heritage road bridges by:
	《安大略省文物桥梁准则》旨在通过以下方式为省级文物桥梁的保护提供指导:
	o
	Establishing a process for their identification, evaluation and listing at an 建立一个过程，以确定，评估和列出
	early stage of the planning process,
	规划过程的早期阶段,
	o
	Identifying conservation options to be considered when planning for any 确定规划时应考虑的保育方案
	rehabilitation, widening or replacement that may be required, 可能需要进行的复修、扩阔或更换工程,
	o
	Identifying the methods and principles for defining heritage values and assessing project alternatives in the Environmental Assessment process, 确定在环境评估过程中界定文物价值和评估工程项目替代方案的方法和原则,
	and
	还有
	o
	Ensuring the management of heritage bridges conforms to the provisions of the Ontario Heritage Act (OHA), the Environmental Assessment Act and its regulations, as well as Ontario Regulation 104/97.
	确保遗产桥梁的管理符合《安大略遗产法》、《环境评估法》及其条例以及安大
	略第 104/97 号条例的规定。
	The Guidelines are intended to be used by:
	本指引旨在供以下人士使用:
	o
	Ministry of Transportation (MTO} staff, including engineers and planners o MTO
	consultants, including engineering and heritage consultants 交通部(MTO)的工作人员，包括工程师和 MTO 顾问的规划师，包括工程师和遗

	o Municipal Heritage Committees and other heritage stakeholders, concerned with the conservation of heritage bridges.
	O 与保护文物桥梁有关的市政文物委员会及其他文物持份者。
	The OHBG provide a clear process for identifying heritage road bridges in a systematic and comprehensive fashion during the early phases of any planning and design work, where the proposed undertaking may affect a road bridge. At this time, the Guidelines are not intended to be used for culverts, since the unique features of culverts are not captured in the current scoring system.
	在任何规划和设计工作的早期阶段，如果拟议的工作可能影响到一座公路桥梁，则
	人力资源管理办公室为系统和全面地确定遗产公路桥梁提供了一个明确的程序。目前，
	该指南并不打算用于涵洞，因为涵洞的独特特征在当前的评分系统中没有体现出来。
	 1.1
	 Introduction
	 1.1 简介
	In 1983, the Ministry of Transportation (MTO) 1 and the Ministry of Culture (MCL)2
	established a Heritage Road Bridges Policy and Ontario Heritage Bridge Guidelines (OHBG). The current document revises and continues the applicability of the OHBG
	for bridges under provincial ownership. Previous versions of the OHBG are superseded as of the publication date of this document.
	1983 年，交通部(MTO)1 和文化部(MCL)2 制定了遗产道路桥梁政策和安大略省遗产桥
	梁准则(OHBG)。目前的文件修订和继续适用的 OHBG 桥梁下的省所有权。以前版本
	的 OHBG 在本文件发表之日被取代。
	Bridges are important parts of our engineering and architectural heritage. Perhaps more than any other type of structure built by man, they exhibit major historical change and innovation in the development and use of materials, in 桥梁是我们工程和建筑遗产的重要组成部分。也许比任何其他类型的人类建造的结
	构，它们展示了重大的历史变化和创新，在发展和使用的材料

	设计及施工方法。它们可以被视为重要的元素，对周围的环境做出积极的贡献。在某
	些情况下，他们是一个重要桥梁类型的罕见幸存者，或者因为他们的年龄，历史联系
	或其他公众认可的价值观而受到尊敬。
	Many bridges, through sensitive rehabilitation , can continue to serve traffic in an efficient and cost-effective way, while retaining their appearance and heritage attributes. Where bridges can no longer serve their original purpose, they may sometimes be adaptively re-used as recreational resources or tourist attractions. For all these reasons, heritage bridges are worth conserving .
	许多桥梁，通过敏感的修复，可以继续以一种有效率和成本效益的方式服务于交通，
	同时保留它们的外观和遗产属性。当桥梁不再能满足其原有的使用目的时，它们有时
	可能被适应性地重新用作娱乐资源或旅游景点。出于所有这些原因，传统桥梁值得保
	护。
	The Guidelines commence with an overview of legislative requirements for bridges in Ontario and the relationship between the Ministry of Transportation and Ministry of Culture under these Guidelines. The process for identifying , evaluating and listing bridges of cultural heritage value, conservation options and the management of heritage bridges is then presented . The Ontario Heritage Bridge List is provided in Appendix A.
	准则首先概述了安大略省桥梁的立法要求以及运输部和文化部在准则下的关系。然后
	介绍了识别、评估和列出文化遗产价值、保护选择和遗产桥梁管理的过程。附录 a 提
	供了安大略省遗产桥梁清单。
	These Guidelines do not supersede any legislation , regu lation or order in council developed by the Ministry of Culture (e.g. Standards and Guidelines for Provincial Heritage Properties and Standards and Guidelines for Consulting Archaeolog ists).
	这些准则并不取代文化部制定的任何立法、法规或理事会命令(如省级文物财产标准和
	准则以及考古学家咨询标准和准则)。
	Retaining the appearance of bridges is of value as articulated in The Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code (CAN/CSA-S6-06), Clause 1.4.2.8, which states: 如加拿大公路桥梁设计规范(CAN/CSA-S6-06)第 1.4.2.8 条所述，保留桥梁的外观是
	有价值的，该条规定:
	"In the design and the rehabilitation of structures, consideration shall be given to the appearance of the finished structure and its compatibility with the surroundings. Wherever possible, the appearance of a structure shall be such that it will be generally perceived as an enhancement to its surroundings."
	“在结构的设计和修复中，应考虑完工结构的外观及其与周围环境的
	兼容性。在任何可能的情况下，结构的外观应该被普遍认为是对周
	围环境的改善。”
	In addition to compatibility with its surroundings, when rehabilitating or replacing a heritage bridge, the design is to reflect or draw upon the heritage attributes outlined by the heritage evaluation documentation.
	除了与周围环境相容之外，在修复或更换遗产桥梁时，设计还要反映或利用遗产评估
	文件概述的遗产属性。
	Where a bridge is known to be Listed, its status must be considered in the environmental assessment process as outlined in Section 4.3.2 of the Class Environmental Assessment Process for Transportation Facilities. Due regard should be given to the heritage attributes of the Listed bridge and its associated landscape by considering the various conservation options described in these Guidelines. Similar regard should be given to structures that may be eligible to be Listed as described in Section 3.0-2.
	如果已知某座桥梁已列入清单，则必须在运输设施类别环境评估程序第 4.3.2 节所概
	述的环境评估程序中考虑其地位。应充分考虑上市桥梁的文物属性及其相关景观，并
	考虑本指引所述的各种保育方案。对于可能有资格列入第 3.0-2 节所述名单的结构，
	也应给予类似的考虑。

	2.0 Legislative Requirements and Heritage Bridges
	2.0 法例规定及文物桥梁
	The Ministry of Transportation is bound by several pieces of legislation when undertaking a project or activity that involves a bridge (40 years old or older): 交通部在开展涉及桥梁(40 年或以上)的项目或活动时受到若干法律的约束:
	•
	The provincial interest in cultural heritage and its conservation is expressed in the Ontario Heritage Act, which provides the legislative framework for heritage conservation in Ontario. The Act is administered by the Ministry of Culture. Part 111.1 of the Act specifically enables the Minister of Culture to develop standards and guidelines for provincially owned properties that have cultural heritage value or interest.
	省级对文化遗产及其保护的兴趣表现在安大略省遗产法案中，该法案为安大
	略省的遗产保护提供了立法框架。该法案由文化部管理。该法案的第
	111.1 部分特别允许文化部长为省级拥有的具有文化遗产价值或利益的财
	产制定标准和指导方针。
	•
	The conservation of cultural heritage resources is also identified as a matter of provincial interest under the Planning Act and the Provincial Policy Statement.
	根据《规划法》和《省政策声明》 ，文化遗产资源的保护也被确定为省级
	关心的事项。
	•
	The Environmental Assessment Act provides for the protection, conservation and wise management of Ontario's environment. The Act defines "environment" in a broad sense that includes natural, social, cultural, economic and built components. This broad definition of the environment makes the assessment of the impact of undertakings on cultural heritage resources part of the standard environmental assessment process in Ontario (improvements to bridges are considered undertakings).
	环境评估法案规定了安大略省环境的保护、保护和明智的管理。该法定义的
	“环境”在广义上，包括自然，社会，文化，经济和建设的组成部分。环境
	的这一广泛定义使得评估企业对文化遗产资源的影响成为安大略省标准环境
	评估进程的一部分(桥梁的改进被视为企业)。
	•
	The Public Transportation and Highway Improvement Act, Ontario Regulation 104/97
	addresses the design, construction and maintenance of bridges .
	《安大略省公共交通和公路改善法》第 104/97 条规定了桥梁的设计、建造和维
	护。
	The provincial interest in cultural heritage is further expressed through Ontario'sparticipation in the Historic Places Initiative, a federal-provincial-territorial partnership with the objective of encouraging heritage conservation across Canada.
	安大略省参与了”历史地点倡议”，这是一个联邦-省-地区伙伴关系，目的是
	鼓励在加拿大全境保护文化遗产，从而进一步表明了该省对文化遗产的兴趣。
	Every bridge project should also be assessed for archaeological impacts around the bridge (e.g. alterations or soil disturbance to surrounding ground or around the base of the bridge). As required by the Ontario Heritage Act and its regulations, all archaeological fieldwork must be undertaken by a licensed archaeologist. A licence is required to alter or remove an artefact or any other physical evidence of past human use or activity from a known land or marine archaeological site.
	每个桥梁工程项目亦应评估桥梁周围的考古影响(例如对周围地面或桥梁底部的改
	动或泥土干扰)。根据《安大略省遗产法》及其法规的要求，所有考古田野工作必
	须由持证考古学家承担。在已知的土地或海洋考古遗址内更改或移走有关人类过
	去使用或活动的文物或任何其他实物证据，均须领有许可证。

	2.
	 1Standards and Guidelines for Provine/al Heritage Properties
	 1 省级文物建筑标准及指引
	In 2005 the Ontario Heritage Act was amended to include a new Part 111.1 , which enables the Minister of Culture, in consultation with those affected, to prepare Standards and Guidelines that:
	2005 年，对《安大略省遗产法》进行了修订，增加了新的第 111.1 部分，使文化部长
	能够与受影响者协商，制定下列标准和准则:
	a) set out criteria and the process for the identification of provincial heritage properties; and
	(a)制订鉴定省级文物财产的准则和程序; 以及
	b} set standards for the protection, maintenance, use and disposal of such properties.
	(二)订定保护、维修、使用和处置该等物业的标准。
	The Standards and Guidelines (still under development at the time of printing the OHBG) will take the form of an Order-in-Council approved by the Lieutenant Governor in Council and will direct the conservation of cultural heritage property in provincial hands. They are intended to provide a framework to support Ontario government ministries and public bodies in their efforts to conserve and protect cultural heritage resources in their ownership or control.
	标准和准则(在印制人权高专办办公室时仍在制定中)将采取副总督会议批准的枢密令的
	形式，并将指导省级手中的文化遗产财产的保护工作。它们旨在提供一个框架，支持
	安大略省政府各部委和公共机构努力保护和保护其所有权或控制的文化遗产资源。
	Within this framework, it is MTO'sresponsibility to ensure the appropriate conservation and management of its heritage bridges. The Ontario Heritage Bridge Guidelines serve this function.
	在这个框架内，MTO 有责任确保其遗产桥梁的适当保护和管理。安大略省遗产
	桥梁指南起到了这个作用。
	As a central principle, the OHA Standards and Guidelines for Provincial Heritage state: "provincial heritage property ... shall be conserved. " It is recognized that the term "conservation" can take on many forms as described in Section 4.3.
	作为一项核心原则，OHA 省级遗产标准和指南规定: “省级遗产财产... 应予保护。”人
	们认识到，术语“保护”可以采取多种形式，如第 4.3 节所述。

	 2.2 交通部和文化部之间的关系
	The Ministry of Transportation'sresponsibilities as a property owner are: 交通部作为财产所有者的职责是:
	•
	To protect the provincial interest in preserving its cultural heritage resources; 保护省级在保护其文化遗产资源方面的利益;

	To identify bridges of provincial importance and conserve them appropriately; 识别省级重要桥梁，并予以适当保护;
	•
	To follow the requ irements of the Ontario Heritage Act, the Environmental Assessment Act and its regulations, and any standards and guidelines for provincial property approved by cabinet under Part 111.1 of the OHA.
	遵循《安大略省遗产法》、《环境评估法》及其规章制度的要求，以及内阁根据 OHA
	第 111.1 部分批准的省级财产的任何标准和准则。
	These responsibilities are met through the application of the processes described in these Guidelines.
	这些责任是通过应用本准则中描述的过程来履行的。
	The Ministry of Culture will:
	文化部将:
	•
	Monitor and review the effectiveness of these guidelines, in consultation with MTO, in meeting the purposes for which they were developed;

	The key element in the management of heritage bridges is the identification of those bridges of provincial importance in MTO's ownershipat an early stage in the planning process . Whenever a bridge is slated for modification, rehabi litation, replacement, or transfer of ownership, MTO must first determine if the bridge has provincial importance and is an important resource due to its cultural heritage value.

	"provincial importance". The scoring system has been developed and calibrated to reflect this; and a score of 60 or greater means that a bridge has provincial importance. Only when a bridge has provincial importance do the conservation options in the Guidelines apply.

	 3.2
	 Evaluation Criteria
	 3.2 评估准则
	The scoring system was developed to provide a clear and easily understood system to identify heritage bridges and assist MTO in setting priorities for the long-term management of its heritage bridges (see Appendix B). Derived from Regulation 9/06
	under the Ontario Heritage Act, the scoring is divided into three main areas: Design/
	Physical Value, Contextual Value and Historic/ Associative Value. Within these three divisions are criteria that are individually scored. To achieve the minimum score of 60
	a bridge must receive scoring in all three divisions. As described in Section 3.0, a bridge that scores 60 or greater is considered a "significant" cultural heritage resource and worthy of inclusion on the Heritage Bridge List. Once a bridge is Listed, the conservation options described in these Guidelines must be applied in the management, planning and EA processes.
	建立评分制度是为了提供一个清晰易懂的制度，以识别文物桥梁，并协助管理处订定
	文物桥梁长远管理的优先次序(见附录 b)。根据《安大略省遗产法》第 9/06 条，评分
	分为三个主要领域: 设计/物理价值、背景价值和历史/联想价值。在这三个部分是单独
	评分的标准。为了达到 60 分的最低分，一座桥必须在所有三个部门得分。如第 3.0 节
	所述，一座得分在 60 分或以上的桥梁被认为是一个“重要的”文化遗产资源，值得列
	入文化遗产桥梁名单。一旦一座桥被列入名单，这些指南中描述的保护选项必须应用
	于管理、规划和环境影响评估过程中。
	The score of a bridge is not a relative indicator to be used to compare Listed bridges against one another. The threshold of 60 has been determined through an extensive calibration process and is used to assess and demonstrate the unique sensitivity of a heritage structure.
	桥梁的得分不是用来比较列出的桥梁之间的相对指标。60 的阈值是通过广泛的校准

	A Cultural Heritage Evaluation Report examines a property as a whole, its relationship to its surroundings, as well as its individual elements- engineering works, landscape and archaeological areas.
	一份文化遗产评估报告审查一个财产作为一个整体，它与周围环境的关系，以及它的个别元素-工程工程，景观和考

	•物业说明、文化遗产价值说明及文化遗产资源(桥梁、文化遗产景观、考古遗址(如有的话)的文物属性说明;
	• Images and supporting documentation.
	•图片和辅助文件。
	The quality of the report depends on a thorough understanding of the physical values of the property, a documentation of its history through scholarly research,and an analysis of its social context. The author of the report relies on standard historical techniques, such as site visits, public consultations, archival research,comparisons with similar properties and mapping.
	报告的质量取决于对财产的实际价值的透彻理解，通过学术研究记录其历史，以及对其社会背景的分析。报告的作者依赖于标
	准的历史技术，如实地考察，公众咨询，档案研究，与类似财产的比较和绘图。
	The preparation of a Cultural Heritage Evaluation Report is the means by which a property is examined for its cultural heritage value. The report is the main source used by MTO and MCL to determine whether a property is of cultural heritage value. If a property is determined to be of cultural heritage value, the report serves as the foundation for the Statement of Cultural Heritage Value and a Conservation Manual for the property. The report also functions as a background document for property managers because it contains useful information about the history of the property and its individual assets.
	文化遗产评估报告的准备是一个财产的文化遗产价值检查的手段。报告是 MTO 和 MCL 用来确定一个财产是否具有文化遗产价
	值的主要来源。如果某物业被确定具有文化遗产价值，该报告将作为该物业的文化遗产价值声明和保护手册的基础。这份报告
	也可以作为物业管理人员的背景文件，因为它包含了有关物业历史和个人资产的有用信息。

	保存: 确定、保护、使用和/或管理文化遗产和考古资源，以保留其遗产价值、属性和完整性。这可以通过保护计划或遗产影响
	评估来解决。(PPS，2005)
	Cultural heritage landscape: A defined geographical area of heritage significance which has been modified by human activities and is valued by a community. It involves a grouping(s) of individual heritage features such as structures, spaces,archaeological sites and natural elements, which together form a significant type of heritage form, distinctive from that of its constituent elements or parts. Examples may include, but are not limited to, heritage conservation districts designated under the Ontario Heritage Act; and villages, parks, gardens, battlefields, mainstreets and neighbourhoods, cemeteries, trailways and industrial complexes of cultural heritage value.(PPS, 2005) 文化遗产景观: 一个确定的地理区域的遗产意义，已经修改的人类活动和社区的价值经纬度。它涉及个别文物特色的分类，例
	如建筑物、空间、考古地点和自然元素，这些特色共同构成一种重要的文物形式，与其组成元素或部分不同。例子可包括但不
	限于根据《安大略遗产法》指定的文物保护区; 以及具有文化遗产价值的村庄、公园、花园、战场、主要街道和街区、墓地、
	铁路和工业园区。(PPS，2005)
	Cultural heritage value: A property may be determined to be of cultural heritage value if it meets the following criteria(derived from The Ontario Heritage Act,Regulation 9/06):
	文化遗产价值: 如果财产符合下列标准，就可以确定其具有文化遗产价值(源自《安大略遗产法》 ，第 9/06 条) :
	• The property has design or physical value because it:
	财产具有设计或物理价值，因为:
	o is a rare, unique, representative or early example of a style, type,expression, material or construction method, o displays a high degree of craftsmanship or artistic merit. or o demonstrates a high degree of technical or scientific achievement.
	O 是一种罕见的、独特的、具有代表性的或早期的风格、类型、表现形式、材料或建筑方法的例子，o 展示了高度的技艺或艺
	术价值。表现出高度的技术或科学成就。
	• The property has historical or associative value because it: 该物业具有历史或联想价值，因为:
	o has direct associations with a theme, event, belief, person, activity,organization or institution that is significant to a community, o yields, or has the potential to yield, information that contributes to an understanding of a community or culture, or o demonstrates or reflects the work or ideas of architect, builder,designer or theorist who is significant to a community.
	O 与对社区有重要意义的主题、事件、信仰、人、活动、组织或机构有直接联系，o 产生或有可能产生有助于理解社区或文化
	的信息，o 展示或反映对社区有重要意义的建筑师、建筑师、设计师或理论家的工作或想法。

	Listed bridge: A bridge that has been identified as having cultural heritage importance, scored greater than 60 in the evaluation, and is worthy of conservation by inclusion on the Ontario Heritage Bridge List. Such bridges are subject to the provisions of the Ontario Heritage Bridge Guidelines.
	列入名录的桥梁: 已被确定为具有文化遗产重要性的桥梁，在评价中得分超过 60 分，并被列入安大略省遗产桥梁名
	录，值得保护。这些桥梁受《安大略省遗产桥梁指南》的规定约束。
	Maintenance: The continuous protective care of the fabric and the setting of a place, and is to be distinguished from repair. Repair involves restoration or reconstruction. Maintenance is fundamental to conservation and should be undertaken where the fabric is of cultural heritage value and its maintenance is necessary to retain that value.
	(The Surra Charter, 1999)
	维护: 对建筑结构和场所的设置进行持续的保护性护理，与维修区别开来。维修包括修复或重建。维修是保护的基
	础，应该在具有文化遗产价值的地方进行，而维修对于保持这种价值是必要的。(The sura Charter，1999) Reconstruction means returning a place to a known earlier state and is distinguished from restoration by the introduction of new material into the fabric.(The Burra Charter, 1999) 重建意味着将一个地方恢复到已知的早期状态，与通过在织物中引入新材料进行修复的区别。(The Burra
	Charter，1999)
	Rehabilitation: In an engineering context, rehabilitation is defined as a modification, alteration or improvement to the existing condition of a structure or bridge subsystem that is designed to correct deficiencies for a particular design life and live load level.(Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code).
	康复: 在工程环境中，康复被定义为对结构或桥梁子系统的现有状况进行修改、改造或改进，以纠正特定设计寿命
	和活荷载水平的不足。(加拿大公路桥梁设计规范)。
	As described in the Standards and Guidelines for the Conservation of Historic Places in Canada, Parks Canada Rehabilitation means the action or process of making possible a continuing or compatible contemporary use for a cultural heritage property, or of an individual component, through repair, alterations and/or additions, while protecting its cultural heritage value.
	如《加拿大历史地点保护标准和准则》所述，加拿大公园修复是指通过维修、改建和/或增建，使文化遗产财产或个
	别组成部分的当代用途得以持续或兼容的行动或过程，同时保护其文化遗产价值。
	Replication: The making of an exact copy of an existing structure feature or artefact.The purpose of replication is usually to replace a missing or decayed component in order to maintain aesthetic unity and harmony.
	复制: 对现有结构特征或人工制品进行精确复制。复制的目的通常是为了替换缺失或腐朽的部件，以保持审美的统
	一和和谐。
	Restoration: The activity in which a structure is returned to the appearance of an earlier time by removing later material and/ or by replacing missing elements and details. Restoration of missing elements must be accurate, use the same materials, and be based on physical evidence or documentary evidence such as historic photographs and drawings.
	修复: 通过移除后来的材料和/或替换缺失的元素和细节，使一个结构恢复到以前的样子的活动。修复缺失的元素必
	须是准确的，使用相同的材料，并基于物理证据或文件证据，如历史照片和图纸。

	Preservation means maintaining the fabric of a place in its existing state and retarding deterioration.(The Burra Charter, 1999) 保存意味着保持一个地方的结构在其现有状态和延缓恶化
	Sympathetic Modification: Means making new work physically and visually compatible with the heritage attributes of a bridge.
	New additions, alterations,structural reinforcements, or related new construction shall not destroy historic materials that characterize the bridge. The new work shall be differentiated from the old and shall be compatible with the massing, size, scale, and architectural features to protect the cultural heritage value of the bridge and its environment.
	同情修改: 意味着使新的工作物理上和视觉上兼容桥梁的遗产属性。新的增建、改建、结构加固或相关的新建筑不得破坏具有
	桥梁特征的历史材料。新工程应与旧工程区别开来，与桥梁的体积、尺寸、规模和建筑特征相适应，以保护桥梁及其环境的文
	化遗产价值。
